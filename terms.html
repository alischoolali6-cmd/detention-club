Terms of Sale

1. Orders

When you place an order on this website you are asking Detention Club to sell you the items in your cart. We confirm your order by giving you an order code and by contacting you on the phone number or email you provide. We may decline or cancel an order, for example if an item cannot be supplied or we cannot reach you to arrange delivery.

2. Prices and payment

All prices are in Bahraini dinars (BD) and fils. Payment is cash on delivery: you pay the driver when your order arrives. The delivery fee depends on your delivery area and is shown at checkout, or confirmed with you by phone before delivery. We do not collect card or bank details on this website.

3. Stock and pre-orders

Items shown as in stock are prepared first. If an item is out of stock you can still order it as a pre-order. Pre-order items usually arrive within 8-10 days. If your order has both in-stock and pre-order items, the delivery timing for each is shown on your tracking page.

4. Delivery and tracking

We deliver within Bahrain using a delivery driver. After you order you get an order code. Enter it on the Track Order page at any time to see the status of your order. Please keep your phone with you so the driver can reach you, and have cash ready.

5. Cancellations

Contact us at the email above as soon as possible if you want to cancel. We can cancel an order that has not yet been sent for delivery.

6. Genuine orders only

Every order must be a real order placed in good faith by someone who intends to accept and pay for it. By placing an order you confirm that your name, phone number and address are correct and that you will be available to receive it.

If you place a false, prank or fake order, or refuse an order you placed without a valid reason, Detention Club may charge a fee of BD 50 to cover the cost caused to us, and may take legal action under the laws of the Kingdom of Bahrain. You must tick the box at checkout to confirm you understand this.

7. Reviews

After your order is delivered you may leave a review. We only publish reviews from real, delivered orders, and we may remove a review that is abusive or untrue.

8. Contact

For questions, problems with an order, inquiries and birthday party orders, email detentionclub0@gmail.com.

Privacy Policy

What we collect

  • When you order: your name, phone number, email address, delivery address, any notes you add, and what you ordered.
  • When you leave a review: your rating and comment. We show only your first name.
  • When you visit: anonymous visit statistics (the page, the type of device and where you came from). We do not use advertising trackers.

Why we use it

We use your details only to process and deliver your order, to contact you about it, to send your delivery update email, and to run and improve the shop.

Who sees it

Only the Detention Club team can see your order details. The delivery driver is given the minimum needed to deliver: your name, phone number, delivery location and the amount to collect. We do not sell your data or share it for marketing.

Where it is stored and for how long

Order data is stored securely with our database provider, Supabase. About 3 months after an order is finished (delivered or cancelled), we delete your name, phone number, email address, delivery address and notes from it. We keep only the order totals and items, with no personal details, for our business records.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you by emailing detentionclub0@gmail.com. We will deal with your request in line with the data protection laws that apply in Bahrain.
